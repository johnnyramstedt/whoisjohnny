What is this?

A growing library of small texts, made as an experiment. I wanted to see what happens when you make something digital, put it out, and let it travel. Each text is a small work, in the same deadpan style as my paper works.

Is it art?

Yes. It is the same practice as my wall works, in a different material: a sentence in plain type that says something a little too directly. Here the material is a file instead of paper. You print it, hang it, or post it. The work is finished when someone uses it.

Why?

Mostly to try. To make something digital, to play with what is possible, and to make life a little more fun, for me and for whoever finds it. And to share the fun.

Who writes the texts?

An AI writes them, and I choose which ones go in the library. That is part of the experiment: to see what happens when a person and a machine make small things together, and to say so openly.
