Questions

What is a Nobody?

A small figure with a name and a number. Drawn by Johnny, assembled by a machine. No two are the same, and no one ever gets made twice.

Why would I want one?

No good reason. It does not do anything. It just stays. Some people collect them. Some give them away. Some set one as their profile picture and forget about it. All of these are correct.

Can I give one as a gift?

Yes. Adopt it, then send the pictures or the link. Put their city in the register if you like.

What are the rare ones?

Every Nobody has traits. Under each one you see how often a trait turns up, for example "Crown · 1 in 14". The price is the same for all of them.

Can I swap or sell it?

No. Once it is yours, it stays yours. It has nowhere else to go.

Where do I post it?

Anywhere. Tag @whoisjohnny2020 on Instagram or @whoisjohnny_2020 on TikTok and we may show it.
