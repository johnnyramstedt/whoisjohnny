Questions

What is a Nobody?

A small figure with a name and a number. Drawn by Johnny, assembled by a machine. No two are the same, and no one ever gets made twice.

Why would I want one?

No good reason. It does not do anything. It just stays, and makes an ordinary day slightly less ordinary. Some people collect them. Some give them away. Some set one as their profile picture and forget about it. Some print the poster and hang it in the hallway. All of these are correct.

How does giving one work?

Pick a Nobody and choose "As a gift". Write their name, their city and a short note. If you add their email, we send it to them right away. If not, you get the gift link and send it yourself, in a message, a card or a text. Either way it is theirs from the start, and it shows up on the map in their city.

How do I print the poster?

Use A4 matte paper, 200–250 g/m², print at actual size (100 %) in best quality, borderless if your printer can. On US Letter, choose "Fit to page". No printer? Any print shop or photo kiosk can print the PDF on A4 matte for a euro or two. It fits a standard A4 frame, and looks best in a 30 × 40 cm frame with a white mat.

Can I collect them?

Yes. Adopt as many as you like. Each one is different, and some traits are rarer than others. Once the shelf is empty of a kind, there will never be another one quite like it.

What are the rare ones?

Every Nobody has traits. Under each one you see how often a trait turns up, for example "Crown · 1 in 14". The price is the same for all of them.

Can I swap or sell it?

No. Once it is yours, it stays yours. It has nowhere else to go.

Where do I post it?

Anywhere. On your Nobody's page you get pictures sized for Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and Facebook (posts, stories and profile pictures). Tag @whoisjohnny2020 on Instagram or TikTok and we may show it.

Thank you

One moment.

Your Nobody is on its way. This usually takes a few seconds.

About the Nobodies.

A Nobody is a small figure with a name, a number and nowhere in particular to be. Johnny draws the parts: heads, hats, bodies, moods. A machine puts them together. Every combination is made once and never again, so each Nobody exists exactly one time, with one owner, in one city.

They are not useful. That is the point. Most things want something from you. A Nobody just stands there, slightly out of place, and makes an ordinary day a little less ordinary.

What people do with them

  • Set it as a profile picture. It fits Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and Facebook. Nobody will ask why. Some will.
  • Post it in a story or use it as a lock screen. Tag @whoisjohnny2020 on Instagram or TikTok, and we may share it.
  • Print the poster. A4, at home or at any print shop. Hallway, office, fridge, the back of a door.
  • Give one away. To a colleague, a cousin, someone who has everything. It is the least necessary present there is.
  • Collect a few. Some traits are rare. Once a Nobody is gone from the shelf, it does not come back.
  • Watch the map. Every adopted Nobody becomes a dot in its owner's city. Slowly, they are spreading.

About the project

WhoIsJohnny? is an art project by Johnny Ramstedt, a Helsinki-based artist. It is a small experiment in owning something that does nothing, and in seeing how far they travel.
