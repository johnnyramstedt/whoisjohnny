WhoIsJohnny?

A project about who gets to be somebody.

Drawings of anonymous figures by Johnny Ramstedt. Series: Pantheon, 2026.

The story

How little does it take to become somebody?

WhoIsJohnny? is a project built around a question, and it has no plan to answer it. Who is he? Who is anyone? Who decides who gets to be somebody?

The first answer is a crowd. Pantheon is a growing gathering of anonymous figures, each with a small head, a few marks for a face and a body of scribble, drawn on paper in different sizes. They are called Quidam, Latin for “a certain someone”: the person nobody bothered to name. Give them a grand title and a number and they start to look important anyway.

It is a serious question dressed in a silly face. Look long enough and the figures look back, which is exactly the point.

Johnny

Johnny Ramstedt (b. 1970, Borgå, Finland) is a visual artist based in Helsinki. He has exhibited internationally since 1999, and his work is in private and corporate collections across Europe and the US.
