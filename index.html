How to print

For a good result on your wall.

Short version: print on matte, uncoated or lightly coated paper, at 100% size, in colour.

A4 at home or at the office
Use matte paper of 120 to 170 g/m². Ordinary copy paper (80 g) works and gives a pleasantly dry look that suits the texts, but thicker paper holds colour better. Choose "Actual size" or "100%" in the print dialog. Do not choose "Fit to page", it adds margins and shrinks the piece.
50 × 70 cm at a print shop
Ask for a poster print on matte paper of 170 to 250 g/m². Matte avoids glare from windows and lamps. Send the 50 × 70 cm PDF as it is and ask for no scaling, no cropping and no extra border. Most shops can print this size in a day.
Black and yellow pages
Some texts have solid black or solid yellow backgrounds. Home inkjet printers can show streaks on large dark areas. A laser printer or a print shop gives the cleanest result. If your printer streaks, try the white versions first.
Sharp at any size
The A4 and 50 × 70 cm files are PDFs with vector text and shapes, so they stay sharp at any print size. The Instagram and TikTok files are pixel images made for screens. Do not print those on paper.
Colour
Files are made in sRGB, which is what most printers and shops expect. Colours can vary slightly between screens and printers. That is normal and is part of the charm.
Frames
A4 and 50 × 70 cm are common frame sizes. Frames are not included. A thin black or white frame suits both the figures and the texts.
